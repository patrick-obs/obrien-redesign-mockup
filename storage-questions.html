Storage questions, answered plainly

Short answers to the questions people ask most about mobile shelving, lockers, vertical lifts, casework and collections storage. If yours is not here, call 610.825.3405.

Mobile and high-density storage

What are movable shelves called?

Most people call them mobile shelving, compact shelving or high-density storage. Rolling shelves, moving-aisle shelving and rail shelving mean the same thing: rows of storage on carriages that roll on floor rails.

Mobile or static shelving: how much more fits?

Static shelving needs an aisle in front of every row. A mobile system shares one moving aisle across a group of rows, so the same room often holds up to about twice as much. We measure your room and what you store before giving you a number.

What does a mobile shelving system cost?

Price depends on the size of the room, how many rows there are and how long they run, the drive, what sits on the carriages, and the floor work. We price it after a walk-through so the number reflects your room, not a rule of thumb.

Can mobile shelving go on an upper floor?

Often, yes. Mobile storage concentrates weight, so your engineer of record reviews the floor using the loading data we provide. The structural decision is theirs.

Shelving

Is it cheaper to build shelves or buy them?

For working storage, buying modular steel shelving is usually the better value. It goes in faster than custom carpentry, adjusts as your needs change, and can be moved or converted to mobile storage later. Built-in woodwork makes sense for finished spaces where looks come first.

Can we start with static shelving and go mobile later?

Yes. We often lay out four-post shelving so it can move onto carriages when space runs out, which can spread the cost over two budget years.

Vertical lifts and carousels

When does a vertical lift module beat shelving?

When a parts or supply room is out of space, picking takes too long, or items need to be tracked. If none of those are true, shelving is usually the better buy, and we will tell you so.

What is the difference between a VLM and a vertical carousel?

A VLM stores trays on both sides of a lift that pulls the tray you ask for and delivers it to the opening, and tray spacing adjusts to the height of what you store. A vertical carousel rotates all of its shelves around a loop until the one you need reaches the opening, with fixed shelf spacing.

Lockers and smart lockers

How do smart lockers work?

Each door has an electronic lock tied to software. A person opens an assigned door with a code, badge or phone, and the system logs every deposit and pickup. Doors can be assigned for a day, a shift or permanently.

What does a smart locker system cost?

Cost depends on the number and size of doors, how people open them (code, badge or app), the software, and whether it ties into your badge system. We price it once we know how the lockers will be used.

Evidence storage

What is an evidence locker?

A secure compartment where an officer deposits evidence so the chain of custody is never broken. Once a door closes, only the evidence custodian can open it again.

Pass-through or non-pass-through?

Pass-through lockers sit in a wall between the officer side and the evidence room: the officer loads from the front and the custodian unloads from the secure back. Non-pass-through lockers load and unload from the same side and suit smaller departments or rooms without a shared wall.

Where do police departments store evidence?

In a secure property and evidence room with controlled access, usually behind temporary lockers where officers drop items. Long-term storage is often high-density shelving, drug and weapons cages, and refrigerated lockers for biological evidence.

Casework

What is the difference between casework and cabinetry?

Casework is the commercial term for cabinets and counters built in a factory and installed as a system, usually specified in Division 12 (for example 12 35 53 for laboratory casework). Cabinetry usually means residential or site-built woodwork. Modular casework can be moved and reconfigured when a room changes.

Can modular casework be moved later?

Yes. Modular and mobile units can be rearranged or relocated when departments change, which is a main reason labs and schools choose it over built-ins.

Museum and collections storage

What makes a museum cabinet conservation grade?

Gasketed doors that keep out dust and pests, finishes that do not off-gas, and drawers and shelves that hold objects steady. We review the specification with your collections staff and conservator before anything is ordered.

Can collections storage be planned before the objects are inventoried?

Yes. We start from object types and rough counts, leave room to grow, and refine the layout as the inventory firms up.
