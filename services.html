How Projects Run

Four steps, one team

01 — ASSESS

Free Space Assessment

We walk your space, measure what you store, and find the capacity you didn't know you had. No cost, no obligation.

02 — DESIGN

Layout & Specification

Drawings, equipment load data, and an itemized quote, matched to the right manufacturer line for the job.

03 — INSTALL

Factory-Trained Installation

Our own crews deliver, anchor, and level, coordinated around your operating hours and site rules.

04 — SUPPORT

Service & Relocation

Maintenance, reconfiguration, teardown and moves, even for systems we didn't originally supply.

What We Do After the Sale

Service is the product

Maintenance & repair, any brandWe maintain and repair storage systems we didn't sell, including legacy installed bases from manufacturers no longer in business.
Relocation & reinstallation4-post and case-style systems disassemble and move with your department, file order intact. Offices, records rooms, whole facilities.

Engineer collaborationWe supply equipment loading data and assist your structural engineer; structural determinations stay with the engineer of record.
