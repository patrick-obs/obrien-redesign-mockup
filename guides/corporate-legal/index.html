Corporate and legal storage

Organized for the way you work

Improved organization. Improved security. Improved use of space.

Better ways to store and find information

Organization, security, and space: the right storage keeps files secure, easy to find, and close to the people who need them. Every project starts with an on-site needs analysis. We review your records and retention requirements, look at your space and how your teams work, and design storage that fits your office, from a single file room to a full corporate headquarters.

We design, supply, and install, and our own crews install office storage throughout the Philadelphia region and beyond. When offices move or consolidate, our crews move the records too, keeping files in order from the old space to the new one. We work alongside your records managers, office and facilities managers, IT teams, architects, and general contractors from the first drawing.

Storage for every part of the office

High-density mobile storage

File shelving mounted on carriages that glide along floor rails, so only the aisle you need is open. A records room can fit into up to half the space, and systems can be locked closed for added security.

Rotary cabinets

Two cabinets in one footprint, with shelving on both sides of a rotating core. A quarter turn closes the unit into a locked steel cabinet after hours.

Lateral file cabinets

Lateral files that keep active files organized right where people work, in letter and legal sizes.

Archive and box storage

4-post shelving sized to records boxes for archives, closed files, and retention storage, labeled so every box is easy to find.

Fire-rated cabinets

Fire-rated cabinets for contracts, deeds, and vital records, rated to industry standards.

Smart lockers

Smart lockers for day-use storage, package delivery, and shared equipment in hybrid workplaces, with compartments assigned, unlocked, and tracked electronically.

Personal lockers

Personal lockers for staff, visitors, and touchdown workspaces, with keyed, combination, and electronic lock options.

Mail sorters and mailroom

Sort modules, consoles, and mailroom casework sized to your daily volume, with adjustable pockets that resize and relabel as departments change.

Modular casework

Modular casework for copy rooms, supply rooms, and break rooms, designed to adapt as your office changes.

Supply and IT storage

Wire and steel shelving for office supplies, IT equipment, and spare parts, with open designs that keep inventory visible.

Secure IT cages

Lockable wire partitions for server rooms, IT equipment, and tenant storage, with clear sight lines and airflow.

Office relocations

Moves and reinstalls that keep your files in order, from packing the records to setting up storage in the new space.
