School and campus storage

Ready before the first bell

Improved organization. Improved access. Improved use of space.

Better ways to store and organize a school

Organization, access, and space: the right storage keeps materials organized, easy to reach, and ready for the next lesson. Every project starts with an on-site needs analysis. We review what each department stores, look at your rooms and how they’re used, and design storage building by building and room by room, from a single classroom to a full campus.

We design, supply, and install, and summer is install season. We schedule deliveries and our own crews around the academic calendar so rooms are ready before students return. We work with schools, colleges, and universities throughout the Philadelphia region and beyond, alongside your facilities directors, business offices, architects, and general contractors.

Storage for every part of the school

Textbook and supply rooms

Adjustable 4-post shelving for textbooks, curriculum materials, and supplies, easy to reconfigure each year as courses change.

Classroom casework

Modular laminate casework for classrooms and prep rooms, with units that move and reconfigure when programs change.

Science labs and prep rooms

Steel casework and lab furniture with chemical-resistant work surfaces for science classrooms and prep rooms.

School libraries

Cantilever library shelving for open, browsable stacks that are easy to reconfigure as collections and spaces change.

High-density mobile storage

Shelving mounted on carriages that glide along floor rails, giving registrars and district offices up to twice the records storage in the same space, without new construction.

Student and staff lockers

Hallway and staff lockers with keyed, combination, and electronic lock options, built to stand up to daily student use.

Athletic lockers and equipment

Ventilated team room lockers and equipment storage for athletics programs, with high-density mobile storage for gear rooms.

Music and band rooms

Instrument storage cabinets and shelving for band and orchestra rooms, sized to the instruments they hold.

Art rooms and makerspaces

Flat files, shelving, and casework for art rooms and makerspaces, with drawers for paper and projects in progress.
