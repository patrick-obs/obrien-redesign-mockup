Healthcare and pharmaceutical storage

More room for patient care

Improved organization. Improved security. Improved use of space.

Better ways to store and supply care

Organization, security, and space: the right storage keeps supplies clean, easy to find, and close to where care happens. Every project starts with an on-site needs analysis. We review your inventory, look at your space, workflow, and security needs, and design storage that fits your facility, from a single supply room to a full hospital or lab building.

We design, supply, and install, and our own crews have worked in active healthcare and pharmaceutical facilities, including many health systems, children’s hospitals, and major pharmaceutical companies. We work alongside your supply chain, pharmacy, lab, facilities, and infection prevention teams, plus your architects and engineers, and coordinate every installation around infection control requirements and clinical schedules throughout the Philadelphia region and beyond.

Storage for every part of the facility

High-density mobile storage

Shelving and cabinets mounted on carriages that glide along floor rails, so only the aisle you need is open. Supply and storage rooms can hold up to twice as much in the same space, and systems can be locked closed for added security.

Wire shelving

Chrome, epoxy-coated, and antimicrobial wire shelving for supply rooms, sterile core, and pharmacies. The open design keeps contents visible from every angle and is easy to clean and reconfigure.

Mobile wire shelving

Wire shelving on carriages for sterile supplies and pharmacy inventory, with the space savings of high-density mobile storage and the airflow of open wire.

Polymer shelving

Polymer shelving that can go through a cart washer, for wet areas, sterile processing, and spaces that need frequent cleaning.

Supply carts

Wire carts and shelving on casters that move supplies from central storage to the floor. Bin fronts and labels make par levels easy to check and restock.

Vertical lift modules

Enclosed towers that store trays and bring the right one to an access opening on demand. Access controls and transaction tracking help secure high-value pharmacy and supply inventory.

Vertical carousels

Rotating shelves inside an enclosed unit that bring stored items to a comfortable working height, making the most of tall ceilings in pharmacies and supply rooms.

See it in 3D

Modular drawer cabinets

Modular drawer cabinets and bin shelving with dividers for small supplies, medications, and parts, so every item has its own labeled place.

Controlled-substance cages

Wire partitions that create a lockable, see-through perimeter for controlled substances and high-value inventory. DEA-regulated storage has its own mesh, ceiling, and locking requirements, and we design around them.

Lab casework

Steel casework and chemical-resistant surfaces for labs, with modular pieces that adapt clinical support spaces without construction.

Records and file storage

High-density mobile storage and rotary cabinets for patient records, HR files, and administrative storage, kept secure and close at hand.

Staff and smart lockers

Personal lockers for staff locker rooms, and smart lockers that assign, unlock, and track compartments electronically for shared equipment and package delivery.
