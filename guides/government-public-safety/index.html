Government and public safety storage

Built for accountability

Improved security. Improved accountability. Improved use of space.

Better ways to store, secure, and track

Security, accountability, and space: the right storage keeps evidence, records, and gear secure, easy to find, and ready to audit. Every project starts with an on-site needs analysis. We review your inventory, intake volume, and retention schedules, look at your space and security needs, and design storage that fits your agency, from a single evidence room to a full public safety building.

We design, supply, and install, and our own crews complete background checks and any other clearances each job requires. They’re experienced working inside secure facilities throughout the Philadelphia region and beyond. O’Brien Systems is a Commonwealth of Pennsylvania Small Business (SB) certified vendor, and many of the manufacturers we work with hold GSA contracts, so we can offer purchasing through their GSA schedules where it applies.

Storage for every part of the agency

Pass-through evidence lockers

Officers deposit evidence through the front, and the door locks behind them. Only the evidence custodian can open the compartment from the rear, and electronic locks log every deposit and retrieval.

Refrigerated evidence lockers

Cold storage compartments for biological evidence, built into the same intake system as your other evidence lockers.

Evidence intake rooms

Intake rooms designed as a complete system, with a mix of locker sizes for phones, firearms, and bulky items, plus packaging stations and the long-term storage behind them.

High-density mobile storage

Evidence and records shelving mounted on carriages that glide along floor rails, so only the aisle you need is open. The same room can hold up to twice as much, and systems can be locked closed for added security.

Secure evidence cages

Lockable wire partitions for drugs, firearms, money, and high-value evidence, with clear sight lines and airflow inside a secure perimeter.

Bulk evidence storage

4-post shelving and pallet rack for bicycles, large items, and bulk evidence, organized so every item is easy to find and audit.

Weapons storage and armories

Weapon racks and cabinets with adjustable cradles for long guns, drawers for sidearms and optics, and locking options from key control to electronic access with full audit trails.

Gear lockers

Personal and gear lockers that keep officer equipment ready and accounted for, in locker rooms, ready rooms, and patrol staging areas.

Records storage

High-density mobile storage, rotary cabinets, and 4-post shelving sized to your retention schedules, for case files, court records, and administrative records.

Fire-rated cabinets

Fire-rated cabinets for vital records and irreplaceable documents, rated to industry standards.

Smart lockers

Smart lockers for shared equipment, radios, and property release, with compartments assigned, unlocked, and tracked electronically.

Uniform and supply storage

Wardrobe and uniform cabinets, shelving, and bins for uniforms, supplies, and quartermaster storage.
