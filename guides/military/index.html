Military and defense storage

Built for readiness

Improved readiness. Improved accountability. Improved use of space.

Better ways to store, issue, and track gear

Readiness, accountability, and space: the right storage keeps gear organized, accounted for, and ready to issue. Every project starts with an on-site needs analysis. We review your equipment, your issue and turn-in process, and your security needs, look at your space, and design storage that fits your unit, from a single supply room to a full base facility.

We design, supply, and install, and our own crews complete background checks and any other clearances each job requires. We coordinate installation around operational schedules throughout the Philadelphia region and beyond. Many of the manufacturers we work with hold GSA contracts, so we can offer purchasing through their GSA schedules where it applies, and O’Brien Systems is a Commonwealth of Pennsylvania Small Business (SB) certified vendor.

Storage for every part of the installation

Gear lockers

Assigned, labeled lockers for issued equipment, from TA-50 gear to personal items, sized to what each service member is issued.

Weapons storage and armories

Weapon racks and cabinets with adjustable cradles for rifles, sidearms, optics, and less-lethal equipment, with lock options from key control to electronic access with full audit trails.

High-density mobile storage

Supply room shelving and gear lockers mounted on carriages that glide along floor rails. The same supply room can hold up to twice the gear, and systems can be locked closed for added security.

Supply room shelving

4-post shelving with labeled positions for issue and turn-in, so every item has an assigned place and inventory is easy to count.

Wardrobe and uniform cabinets

Wardrobe cabinets with full-height hanging space for uniforms, outerwear, and gear bags, with keyed, padlock hasp, or electronic locks.

Ready rooms

Gear lockers, benches, and shelving for ready rooms, laid out so service members can gear up quickly.

Secure cages

Lockable wire partitions for sensitive items, high-value equipment, and secure storage, with clear sight lines and airflow.

Vertical lift modules

Enclosed towers that store parts and small items on trays and deliver them on demand, with access controls and transaction tracking for accountability.

Maintenance and parts storage

Modular drawer cabinets, bin shelving, and pallet rack for vehicle and equipment maintenance parts, tools, and bench stock.

Records storage

High-density mobile storage, rotary cabinets, and fire-rated cabinets for personnel files, training records, and administrative records.

Smart lockers

Smart lockers for shared equipment, radios, and package delivery, with compartments assigned, unlocked, and tracked electronically.
