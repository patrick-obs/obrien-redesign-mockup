Library and archive storage

Room for the collection and the community

Improved access. Improved capacity. Improved use of space.

Better ways to store and share a collection

Access, capacity, and space: the right storage keeps a collection easy to browse, well cared for, and ready to grow. Every project starts with an on-site needs analysis. We review your collection, look at your space and how patrons and staff use it, and design storage that fits your library, from a single reading room to a full library building.

We design, supply, and install, and our own crews install library storage throughout the Philadelphia region and beyond. Book stacks are heavy, so we provide complete loading data and work with your facilities team, architects, and structural engineer before anything is ordered. We work alongside your librarians, archivists, and administrators from the first drawing.

Storage for every part of the library

Cantilever library shelving

Classic browsable stacks with no front posts in the way, so books stay front and center. Re-shelving is easy, and finished end panels and canopy tops give the stacks a furniture-quality look.

High-density mobile storage

Shelving mounted on carriages that glide along floor rails, so only the aisle you need is open. The same room can hold up to twice the collection, freeing up floor space for seating, study areas, and educational programs.

Closed stacks and remote storage

High-density mobile storage and 4-post shelving for closed stacks, overflow, and remote storage, so materials stay in the collection without taking up public floor space.

Archives and special collections

Closed-stack and conservation-grade options for rare books, manuscripts, and special collections, including steel cabinets with gasketed doors that help protect against dust and pests.

Flat files and map cases

Flat file cabinets with full-extension drawers for maps, prints, photographs, posters, and oversized materials.

Media and periodical storage

Shelving and display options for DVDs, audiobooks, magazines, and newspapers, sized to the materials and easy to browse.

Children’s and school library shelving

Lower-height shelving, picture book bins, and end-panel finishes for children’s areas and school libraries.

Archive and records shelving

4-post shelving sized to archival and records boxes, for library archives, local history collections, and administrative records.

See it in 3D

Rotary cabinets

Rotary cabinets that store files, media, and supplies on both sides of a rotating unit, for staff workrooms and circulation areas.

Fire-rated cabinets

Fire-rated cabinets for irreplaceable records and materials, rated to industry standards.

Holds pickup lockers

Smart lockers that let patrons pick up holds and borrowed equipment on their own schedule, with compartments assigned and tracked electronically.

Staff workrooms

Worktables, carts, shelving, and modular casework for technical services, processing, and staff workrooms, designed with the storage so materials move a short distance.
