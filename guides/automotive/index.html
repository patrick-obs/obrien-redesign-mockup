Automotive parts and dealership storage

Every part in its place

Improved organization. Improved access. Improved use of space.

Better ways to store and find parts

Organization, access, and space: the right storage keeps parts secure, easy to find, and ready for the counter or the service bay. Every project starts with an on-site needs analysis. We review your parts inventory, look at your space and workflow, and design storage that fits your operation, from a single parts room to a full distribution center.

We design, supply, and install, and we plan your layout around the bin locations in your dealer management system (DMS), so the physical storage matches your inventory records. Our own crews install automotive storage throughout the Philadelphia region and beyond, and we work with national automotive retailers on locations across the country. We work alongside your parts managers, service directors, facilities teams, architects, and general contractors from the first drawing.

Storage for every part of the operation

High-density mobile storage

Parts shelving mounted on carriages that glide along floor rails, so only the aisle you need is open. The same parts room can hold up to twice the inventory, and systems can be locked closed for added security.

Vertical lift modules

Enclosed towers that store small parts on trays and deliver them to the counter at the push of a button. Access controls and transaction tracking help secure and track high-value parts.

Vertical carousels

Rotating shelves inside an enclosed unit that bring parts to a comfortable working height, making the most of tall ceilings behind the parts counter.

Tire racks

Purpose-built racks that organize bulky tire inventory by size, keeping tires off the floor and easy to pull.

Parts shelving

Steel shelving with bin fronts, dividers, and labels for fast-moving parts, laid out to match the bin locations in your dealer management system (DMS).

Modular drawer cabinets

Modular drawer cabinets with dividers for fasteners, filters, and electrical components, holding far more than open shelving in the same footprint.

Long-part racks

Cantilever and vertical racks for exhaust pipes, trim, and other long or awkward parts, so they’re stored flat or upright instead of leaning in a corner.

Pallet rack

Pallet rack for bulk fluids, batteries, and boxed inventory in receiving and back-stock areas.

Mezzanines

Mezzanines that add a second level of parts storage above the existing floor, without adding on to the building.

Secure parts cages

Lockable wire partitions for high-value parts, catalytic converters, and special orders, with clear sight lines and airflow.

Parts pickup lockers

Smart lockers that let customers and technicians pick up parts and special orders after hours, with compartments assigned and tracked electronically.

Technician and shop storage

Personal lockers, worktables, and modular casework for technicians, service advisors, and shop offices, designed with the storage so parts travel a short distance.
