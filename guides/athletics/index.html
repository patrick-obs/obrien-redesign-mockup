Redefined: A team room built around every player, their gear, and your program.
Athletic equipment and team room storage

Built for game day, every day

Improved organization. Improved airflow. Improved use of space.

Better ways to store and organize gear

Organization, airflow, and space: the right storage keeps gear dry, easy to find, and ready for the next practice. Every project starts with an on-site needs analysis. We review your equipment inventory, look at your space and how your teams use it, and design storage that fits your program, from a single team room to a full athletic facility.

We design, supply, and install, and our own crews handle every installation throughout the Philadelphia region and beyond. We work alongside your athletic directors, equipment managers, coaches, architects, and facilities teams so the storage fits how your program runs from the first drawing.

Storage for every part of the program

Athletic team lockers

Open-front and ventilated lockers for the team room, with a place for each player’s helmet, pads, cleats, and personal items. Ventilated designs let gear air out between practices.

High-density mobile storage

Shelving and gear compartments mounted on carriages that glide along floor rails, so only the aisle you need is open. The same equipment room can hold up to twice the gear, and systems can be locked closed for added security.

Custom cradles and holders

Helmet, shoulder pad, stick, and bat holders sized to the sport, so every piece has its own place and nothing is piled in a bin.

Equipment cages

Wire mesh partitions that turn open floor space into lockable cages for balls, training gear, and travel bags, with airflow and visibility from every side.

Wire shelving

Open wire shelving for balls, cones, pads, and training equipment. Open decks let air move around damp gear and make it easy to see what’s on hand.

Mobile wire shelving

Wire shelving on carriages for uniform, laundry, and supply rooms, combining the space savings of mobile storage with the airflow of open wire.

4-post shelving

Heavy-duty shelving for bulk supplies, boxed equipment, and off-season storage, built for a full season of use.

See it in 3D

Uniform storage

Garment rods, shelves, and cabinets that keep game and practice uniforms organized by sport, size, and number, so inventory is easy to count before and after the season.

Secure personal lockers

Lockable storage for coaches, staff, and visiting teams, with keyed, combination, and electronic lock options.

Recruiting-ready finishes

Branded end panels and team colors that turn the equipment room into a stop on the recruiting tour.

Equipment workrooms

Casework, worktables, and cabinets for equipment repair, laundry, and coaches’ offices, designed with the storage so gear moves a short distance.
