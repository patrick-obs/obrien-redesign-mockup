The Case for Compact Storage

We make every inch count

Fixed aisles waste up to half your floor. Putting the same shelving on mobile carriages removes the empty aisles. Same footprint, twice the storage, or the same storage in half the space.

Conventional fixed shelving

Every row needs its own access aisle, so more than half the floor is air.

O'Brien high-density mobile 2× capacity

Carriages glide on rails to open one aisle where you need it. The rest of the floor works for you.
